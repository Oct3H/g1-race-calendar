1.2. Rebate
Rebate applies to each bet placed in Win, Place, Quinella and
          Quinella Place for all local and overseas races (except Outbound
          Commingling).
• Whether it be a bet on a single race, a bet consisted in All Up
          betting^ or a bet consisted in Cross Pool All Up betting^, any
          ticket* or betline** with a total losing bet amount of HK$10,000 or
          above placed on designated pool will be eligible to receive a rebate
          of prescribed percentage of the total losing bet amount
• The rate of rebate for any losing bet consisted in All Up betting^
          and/or Cross Pool All Up betting^ will be determined according to
          the bet type placed for the losing leg, irrespective of the bet type
          for the winning legs in that bet

3. All Up or Cross Pool All Up Betting
You can enjoy greater flexibility by choosing All Up or Cross Pool
          All Up bet. Simply by placing bets in the same or different betting
          pools for 2 races to a maximum of 6 races. Any dividend entitlement
          from a winning Leg will be automatically carried forward and
          reinvested in the subsequent Leg specified in your bet selection.

B. Fixed Odds Bet Types
Jockey Challenge
Pick your favorite jockey and target odds. Dividend is calculated
          based on the fixed odds at the time the bet is accepted and will not
          be affected by any subsequent odds change.
How to Play
Jockeys participating in the nominated races in a Jockey Challenge
          will be numbered 1 to 14. No. 1 to 13 will be assigned to one named
          jockey each, while No. 14 will include the rest of the jockeys.
          Select correctly the jockey with the most Jockey Challenge Points in
          a Jockey Challenge and your winnings will be paid at the price
          taken.
“Jockey Challenge Points” means the points assigned to the jockeys
          actually riding the 1
st
, 2
nd
and 3
rd
horses, respectively, on the Jockey Challenge Scheduled Rides.*
*Means the rides that a jockey is engaged to ride in the nominated
          races at the start selling of Jockey Challenge. The Jockey Challenge
          Scheduled Rides List will be released at the start selling time of
          Jockey Challenge, please refer to the Scheduled Rides List for
          details
In Play Betting Available
In Play Betting is available for Jockey Challenge. Bets may be
          placed until the start of second last race. Selling will be suspended
          at the start of each race, and will resume after the Weighed in
          Signal is displayed. Odds and the Jockey Challenge Points will be
          updated after each race.
Jockey Challenge Points
Jockeys riding the first three placed horses^ on their Scheduled
          Rides will earn Jockey Challenge Points as follows (Dead-heat

Trainer Challenge
Pick your favorite trainer and target odds. Dividend is calculated
          based on the fixed odds at the time the bet is accepted and will not
          be affected by any subsequent odds change.
How to Play
Trainers who saddle up starters in the nominated races in a Trainer
          Challenge will be numbered 1 to 21. No. 1 to 20 will be assigned to
          one named trainer each (up to 20 named trainers), while No. 21 will
          include the rest of the trainers. Select correctly the trainer with
          the most Trainer Challenge Points in a Trainer Challenge and your
          winnings will be paid at the price taken.
“Trainer Challenge Points” refer to the points the trainers scored
          from each of their starters finishing 1
st
, 2

& 3
rd

In Play Betting Available
In Play Betting is available for Trainer Challenge. Bets may be
          placed until the start of second last race. Selling will be suspended
          at the start of each race, and will resume after the Weighed in
          Signal is displayed. Odds and the Trainer Challenge Points will be
          updated after each race.
Trainer Challenge Points
Trainers of the first three placed horses^ will earn Trainer
          Challenge Points as follows (Dead-heat situations excepted):
1
st

2

3

Where a Trainer has more than one starter in a race nominated for
          Trainer Challenge, the Trainer Challenge Points he will score from
          each of his starters in such race will be counted for the Trainer
          Challenge. A Trainer can earn at most 22 points in a race provided
          that the first three placed horses are all from his stable.
The trainer with the highest accumulated Trainer Challenge Points in
          a Trainer Challenge will be declared as the winner. The Trainer
          Challenge Points for all trainers (including the individual trainers
          in No.21 “Others”) will be counted on an individual basis.

C. Dividend Collection
Valid betting tickets must be presented for payment within 60 days
          of the date after the relevant race meeting (Example: Winning
          tickets for races held on 1 March can be presented for payment on
          any day from 1 March to 30 April).
